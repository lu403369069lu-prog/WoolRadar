## Recovery principles

1. A test-only historical commit is not a production fix, and a current source blob containing a similar fix is not proof of full regression coverage.
2. Use `VERIFIED_REMOTE`, `HISTORICAL_TEST_REPORTED`, `ISOLATED_RERUN`, `PR_DRAFT_PATCHED`, `ACCEPTANCE_FAILED`, or `UNVERIFIED_SOURCE` at the granularity of each test/result, not one global `DONE`.
3. **Value is not submission count**: historical patches saved in personal Library can be source-recovered but should only be integrated after comparing newest code. Do not upload private raw reports, SQLite copies, secret keys, source labels or cookies to GitHub.
4. A green 412-code unit suite does not turn independently red 25%-recall into acceptance; a synthetic Ed25519 receiver does not prove real user-approved report delivery. No auto GitHub merges, deployments, paid APIs, Codex or Work.
5. Next highest-value engineering action: run PR37 full official sampler test and the cross-URL false-split/new untouched blind sample quality gate, rather than reissue historical patch copies.
